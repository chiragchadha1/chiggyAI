## The problem

Most AI assistants forget you the moment a session ends. Anlyst was a bet that decision
fatigue gets solved by agents with persistent, personal context, not by another stateless
chatbot.

## What I built

As CTO of a two-person founding team, I architected a three-layer Vault, Mirror, and
Persona system: the Vault holds raw personal data, the Mirror synthesizes it into an
understanding of the user, and the Persona is the agent that acts on it. The MVP pulled real
signal from Whoop, Apple Health, Google Calendar, and MyFitnessPal so the agent reasoned
from your actual life, not generic advice.

## Impact

The launch drove 200+ waitlist sign-ups and 3+ VC conversations before I moved to full-time
engineering at HubSpot. The memory thesis outlived the company. It became the seed for
[chiggyOS](/work/chiggyos), where I rebuilt the same idea as personal infrastructure I own.
