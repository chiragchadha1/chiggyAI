## The problem

Most AI assistants forget you the moment a session ends. Anlyst was a bet that decision fatigue gets solved by agents with persistent, personal
context.

## What I built

As CTO of a two-person founding team, I designed the system in three layers. A **Vault**
pulls your real data from apps like Whoop, Apple Health, Google Calendar, and
MyFitnessPal. A **Mirror** turns raw data into personal context by correlating sleep,
activity, and schedule patterns. And a **Persona** acts on what it knows about you instead
of giving generic advice.

<Diagram name="vault-mirror-persona" />

The whole point was that the agent should reason from your actual life. If you slept poorly
and have a packed calendar, it should know that without you having to say it.

## Impact

The launch got 200+ waitlist sign-ups and early VC conversations before I moved to
full-time engineering at HubSpot. The memory thesis outlived the company. It became the seed
for [chiggyOS](/work/chiggyos), where I rebuilt the same idea as personal infrastructure I
own.
