## The problem

Customers kept finding outdated photos and old menus scattered across the web, which created
real confusion about what the shop actually serves. The store needed one source of truth it
controlled.

## What I built

A Next.js, React, and Framer Motion site designed and built end to end with agentic
development workflows: a homepage with hero, auto-scrolling photos, an about section, and a
catering call to action, a full daily menu page with search, and a catering page with
packages and pricing. Every order button routes to ChowNow, and the whole thing is mobile
responsive on Vercel.
