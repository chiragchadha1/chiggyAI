## The problem

The best tables in NYC open and vanish in seconds. I wanted to learn to code something
genuinely hard, so I started building a bot that could grab them the instant they dropped.

## What I built

It started as a slow browser-automation script. The competitive drops demanded speed, so I
reverse-engineered the booking flow into an API-first tool and added a pre-solve trick
borrowed from sneaker bots: prepare the slow verification step a minute ahead of the drop,
cache the result, then fire the booking the instant the table opened.

<Diagram name="presolve-timeline" />

That took bookings down to under two seconds from drop time. Around the engine I built a
real dashboard: login, customer profiles, request management, and Stripe authorization holds
that only charge on a successful booking.

## What happened

One friend's Instagram story brought 50+ people into my DMs overnight, and I had paying
customers within the week. Instant product-market fit.

## Lessons

The platform risk caught up with me, so I shut it down while it was still working. The build
proved I could ship something people wanted, fast. The shutdown taught the harder lesson:
some things you build in private, and knowing when to walk away is its own skill.
