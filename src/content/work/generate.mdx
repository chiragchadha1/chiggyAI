## What I did

Generate is Northeastern's student-led product development studio. Across three roles I
oversaw programming for an organization of 160+ students, leading engineering direction
while also running content and engagement.

I shipped Couplet, a social networking mobile app, with a 15-person cross-functional team of
engineers, designers, and product managers, and presented it to 300+ Showcase attendees. As
content lead I drove 103K+ impressions through a rebranding campaign.

## Why it mattered

It was where I learned to lead a team rather than just build alone: scoping work for people
with different skills, shipping on a deadline, and turning a room full of students into a
product org that actually ships.
