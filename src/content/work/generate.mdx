## What I did

Generate is Northeastern's student-led product development studio. I held three roles across
my time there: Software Project Lead, Content Lead, and Engagement Director.

As Software Project Lead I led a 15-person cross-functional team of engineers, designers,
and product managers to build **Couplet**, a social app connecting people through shared
interests in local events, owning the full cycle from concept to a 300+ person Showcase. As
Content Lead I ran a rebranding and digital/SEO strategy that drove 103K+ impressions. As
Engagement Director I led three chiefs owning content, events, and experiences for the
160+ person org.

## Why it mattered

It is where I learned to lead rather than just build: scoping work for people with different
skills, shipping on a deadline, and developing talent (including mentoring a freshman who
went on to run eight project teams as Software Director). It turned a room full of students
into a product org that actually ships.
